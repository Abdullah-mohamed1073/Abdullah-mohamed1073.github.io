Projects

Smart car

An obstacle-avoiding robot car. An ultrasonic sensor on a servo scans the way ahead, and an Arduino with a motor driver shield steers the four wheels around whatever it finds.

What I did

  • Assembled the four-wheel chassis and wired the motors to the motor driver shield.
  • Mounted the ultrasonic sensor on a servo so the car can look left and right.
  • Wrote the control code in C/C++ to measure distance and choose a direction.
  • Tested and tuned the car until it avoided obstacles reliably.

Challenges and how I solved them

  • The motors drew more current than the board could supply, so I powered them from a separate source.
  • Ultrasonic readings were noisy, so I averaged several measurements before making a decision.
  • Loose jumper wires caused random resets, so I secured the connections and tested each part on its own first.

  • Servo
  • Motor driver shield

Smart home network

A complete smart home simulated in Cisco Packet Tracer. A Home Gateway connects the IoT devices to the network, and a smartphone or PC controls them through the IoT server's web interface.

What I did

  • Designed the network around a Home Gateway acting as the central hub.
  • Added a smart garage door, dimmable lighting, smart AC units and an automated irrigation system.
  • Connected the devices wirelessly and set up a clear IP addressing scheme.
  • Controlled every device from a smartphone or PC through the IoT server.

Challenges and how I solved them

  • Devices only appeared in the control panel after they were registered with the Home Gateway, so I matched the wireless and gateway settings on each one.
  • Devices with a wrong subnet or a duplicate IP address could not be reached, so I planned the addressing before configuring anything.
  • Testing one device at a time showed me exactly which one failed instead of guessing.
